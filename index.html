The Glance® Smart City System

One platform. Everything connected.

Glance is a cloud platform that connects a city's infrastructure (traffic signals, school beacons, streetlights and vehicles) so agencies can monitor, control and improve them from one place.

  • Monitor and control all connected assets from a single dashboard
  • Automated alerts by SMS and email cut downtime and response time
  • Connected-vehicle ready: C-V2X, 4G LTE and 900 MHz, with 5G compatibility
  • Built for Indian cities, with engineering and support from our Mohali office
